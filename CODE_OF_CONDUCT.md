# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). By taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions you agree to it.

## In short

- **Be kind and assume good faith.** People report bugs and corrections because they care about the mod.
- **Critique ideas, code and data, not people.**
- **No harassment, insults or personal attacks.** Discussing the game's romance content is on topic; directing sexual remarks at other people is not.
- **Mind spoilers in titles.** Put major story spoilers in the body of an issue rather than its title where you can.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, contact the maintainers privately through their GitHub profile rather than replying in public. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and restrict participation for behaviour that breaks this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
